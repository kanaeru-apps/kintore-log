3. Functions, audience, problem, and value
Kintore Log is an iPhone workout log for people who want to record gym sessions quickly. Users can record strength sets (exercise, equipment, weight, repetitions, and memo) and cardio sessions; review history in a calendar; view 1RM and training-volume charts; calculate estimated 1RM; manage exercises; use an interval timer with local notifications; and import/export CSV files. The app reduces data-entry time through drum-style numeric selectors and shows previous-set values for reference.

4. Access and setup instructions
No account, registration, login, demo credentials, subscription, purchase, or sample file is required. Launch the app and tap the + control on the Record tab to add a workout. History, Charts, Timer, Calculator, and Settings are available from the bottom tab bar. To test a timer notification, open Timer, choose a short duration, tap Start, allow notification access when requested, and place the app in the background or lock the device.

5. External services, tools, and platforms
No external service is required for the core workout-recording functionality. Workout records remain on the device. The app uses Apple iOS local notifications, local storage/filesystem, haptics, and the standard share/file interfaces. An optional feedback form sends only the text entered by the user, an optional email address, and the app version to a developer-managed Google Apps Script endpoint. User-initiated links can open a YouTube search, a reference-video URL entered by the user, or the Apple App Store review page. The App Store build has no cloud-sync feature and includes no authentication, payment processor, advertising, analytics, AI service, remote push-notification server, or third-party tracking SDK.

6. Regional differences
The app functions consistently in all regions where it is available. There are no region-specific features or content. The interface is Japanese and workout weights are displayed in kilograms.

7. Regulated industry or protected third-party material
The app is a personal fitness log, not a medical device or medical service. Estimated 1RM values are informational calculations and are not medical advice. The app does not embed or distribute protected third-party content. YouTube is opened only as a user-initiated external search.
